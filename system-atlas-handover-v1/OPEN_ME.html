OWNER INTENTION

Make complex systems understandable and memorable.

Use futuristic depth, light, motion and interaction to explain a real architecture and its activity. Begin with AI training and inference; keep the visual system adaptable to other distributed IT platforms.

APPROVED PRODUCT STRUCTURE

07 for the whole system. 05A for one transaction.

The views share identity, filters, source and time context. Open a selected transaction from the atlas, inspect or demonstrate it, then return to the same system view.

07 · Topographic Atlas

The bird’s-eye view: system boundaries, territories, components and relationships.

SYSTEM → TERRITORY → COMPONENT

Preserve: shallow terrain, clear boundaries, reusable glyphs, amber selected route and cyan response. Implement: grouping, search, stable selection, component inspection and Open transaction.

Contours represent containment. They do not measure latency or imply physical geography. Resolve ambiguous arrows and deployment links from the data model.

05A · Precision Trace

Introspect or demonstrate one transaction, with exact timing and supporting evidence.

SELECT → FOLLOW → INSPECT → RETURN

Preserve: dark spatial composition, precise glass stages, readable labels, amber execution, inspector and replay strip. Implement: span/event inspection, branching, queue wait, streaming evidence and synchronized timeline.

Scene position represents architecture. Exact time belongs in the trace timeline. The focus plane is a navigation cue, not a quantitative global time slice.

Rules that keep the product trustworthy

Visual drama should make real behavior easier to understand.

One source of truth

Same identities in both views

Topology, traces, lineage and metrics remain distinct. Selections and evidence survive view changes.

Honest activity

Demo, recorded and live

Visible source labels, freshness and coverage. Never fill missing spans or response events with invented data.

Real interaction

Build scene objects

The PNGs are references. Rebuild interactive geometry and controls; preserve keyboard, reduced-motion and 2D access.
